# Changelog

Policy changes are listed here with the date they took effect. Typo and link fixes are not listed.

## 0.2.0, 5 October 2026

Tailored to the lab's methods and its institutional position.

- README: added a short version.
- Chapter 1: says where the lab sits and which practices assume a group of five or more.
- Chapters 3 and 12: the lab head's accountability covers the lab's science and how the group works. The institution keeps its legal duties.
- Chapters 3, 4 and 15, and CONTRIBUTING: the anonymous survey applies from five members. Before that, feedback goes through the external contact. The anonymous suggestion form is removed.
- Chapter 5: the external contact must agree to the role. Students and visitors can also use their home institution.
- Chapter 7: new section on human cell lines, stem cells and organoids. Decision criteria are written before the data.
- Chapter 8: human sequencing and genotype data, sequencing and imaging records, and where protocols are shared.
- Chapter 9: software, protocols and datasets are credited.
- Chapter 11: the lab head's outside interests are disclosed to members and the host institution, not listed on the website. The approvals list covers human donor material and genome-edited cells.
- Chapter 12: hazards specific to cell culture, cryogens, viral vectors and developmental toxicants.
- Chapters 13 and 14, and the templates: core facilities, cell culture sign-off, exchange students and human lines.
- Licence file replaced with the full CC BY 4.0 text. Repository address and one citation title corrected.

## 0.1.0, 5 October 2026

First draft, written by the lab head. Covers roles, working together, conduct and concerns, mentoring, research practice, data, authorship, publishing, compliance, safety, operations, and onboarding and leaving. Eight templates added. To be revised with the first lab members before version 1.0.
