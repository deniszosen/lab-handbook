# Keeping this handbook alive

A handbook nobody reads and nobody changes becomes fiction. This chapter says how we stop that.

## Review

The group reviews the handbook twice a year, once in each semester, in a lab meeting set aside for it. Before the meeting, everyone reads one or two chapters chosen in advance, and the lab head collects questions, complaints and suggestions. People who prefer not to say something in the room send it to the external contact, who passes it on without a name. At the meeting we ask three things. Does it say what we actually do? Is there anything we do that it should say? Is there something in it we no longer want?

New members read the whole handbook in their first month and are the best readers for finding what is unclear.

After any incident, a near miss or a dispute that exposed a gap, the lab checks whether the handbook needs a change, whether or not a review is due.

## Who can change it

Anyone in the lab can propose a change, as described in [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md). Wording fixes are merged by the lab head. Changes to a rule or an expectation need discussion at a lab meeting and are merged by the lab head after it. The lab head does not change a rule that affects members without that discussion.

People who do not use GitHub can propose changes by email, or through the external contact described in [chapter 5](05-conduct-and-raising-concerns.md). Those proposals are handled the same way.

## Versions

Version numbers have three parts. A change to a rule or expectation raises the middle number. A fix to wording or a link raises the last one. A rewrite of the structure raises the first. Every version is tagged in the repository, and policy changes are recorded in [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md) with the date they took effect.

When a member joins, they get the current version. Members who have been in the lab for longer are told at lab meeting when something changes.

## Conflicts with other policies

If this handbook disagrees with institutional policy, a contract or the law, those win. Fix the handbook and note it in the changelog.

## Public and private parts

This repository is for text that is safe to publish. Contact details of individuals, internal links, and anything about specific people, disputes or concerns live in the lab's private space. The handbook refers to them by description.
