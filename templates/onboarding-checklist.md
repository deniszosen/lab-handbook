# Onboarding checklist

For the lab head and the new member's buddy. Tick items as they are done.

New member:
Start date:
Buddy:

## Before day one

- [ ] Welcome note sent with start time, who will meet them, and what to bring
- [ ] Contract, institutional registration and access card arranged
- [ ] Workspace, computer and accounts ready
- [ ] Buddy assigned and told
- [ ] Added to the calendar, lab channels and mailing lists
- [ ] Added to the lab's emergency contact list, if they agree
- [ ] Agreement from the home institution received (exchange, Erasmus+ and visiting members)

## Day one

- [ ] Met the group
- [ ] Toured the lab, including exits, extinguishers, eyewash, shower and first aid
- [ ] Accounts and password manager set up with two-factor authentication
- [ ] Told where to find the handbook and which chapters to read first

## First week

- [ ] Read chapters 2, 3, 4, 5 and 12
- [ ] Safety induction completed
- [ ] First one-to-one held
- [ ] Expectations agreement filled in and saved
- [ ] Given the name of an external contact for concerns about the lab head
- [ ] Shown how to book instruments, order supplies, and use the inventory

## First month

- [ ] Read chapters 6 to 9
- [ ] Required training completed and recorded
- [ ] Cell culture sign-off completed before culturing independently
- [ ] Liquid nitrogen and biosafety training completed, if they will work with cells
- [ ] Consent and approvals for any human lines they will use filed in the project folder
- [ ] First project plan written and discussed
- [ ] Data management plan drafted if data will be collected
- [ ] Met the collaborators and facility staff they will work with
- [ ] Gave feedback on the onboarding itself
- [ ] Suggested at least one handbook change, even if small

## Three months

- [ ] Progress and expectations agreement reviewed
- [ ] Individual development plan written
- [ ] Authorship and contribution plan in place for current projects
