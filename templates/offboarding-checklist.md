# Offboarding checklist

For the leaving member and the lab head. Start at least four weeks before the last day.

Leaving member:
Last day:
Lab head sign-off date:

## Planning

- [ ] Last day confirmed and handover plan agreed
- [ ] Remaining work listed: experiments, analyses, drafts
- [ ] Authorship plan reviewed for every unpublished project
- [ ] Agreed who takes over each project, and who the contact person is

## Data and records

- [ ] Raw data in the project folders and backed up
- [ ] Project READMEs and data dictionaries up to date
- [ ] Code in repositories, runnable from the README, with versions recorded
- [ ] Notebooks complete and handed over
- [ ] Sample, strain, cell line and reagent inventories up to date, with locations
- [ ] Cryostocks and cell bank entries transferred to a named person, with the freezer map and thaw log updated
- [ ] Personal copies of data removed from private devices and accounts
- [ ] Approvals and agreements filed in the project folders

## Accounts and access

- [ ] Shared files transferred to a named owner
- [ ] Lab accounts, mailing lists and channels removed or transferred
- [ ] Keys and cards returned
- [ ] Equipment training and booking access closed
- [ ] Institutional accounts closed according to their rules

## Closing

- [ ] Lab space and freezer space cleared and cleaned
- [ ] Unfinished manuscripts: who writes, who reviews, by when
- [ ] Exit conversation held
- [ ] Contact details for questions about the work agreed
- [ ] Reference and alumni arrangements discussed
- [ ] Lab head signed off
